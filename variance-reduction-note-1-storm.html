Variance Reduction Note 1: The Convergence of STORM

Alina Lodhi

Graduate student · University of Agriculture Faisalabad

Technical noteSeptember 1, 20269 pages

Abstract

Stochastic recursive momentum (STORM) reduces gradient estimation error by combining a moving average with a correction evaluated at two consecutive iterates using the same random sample. This tutorial explains that mechanism and gives a self-contained convergence proof for a fixed-parameter, finite-horizon version with a single initialization batch. Under unbiasedness, bounded variance, and mean-square smoothness, the method attains an expected squared gradient norm of order T−2/3 after T iterations, yielding O(ε−3) stochastic gradient evaluations for an expected gradient norm at most ε, with problem parameters fixed. The analysis is pedagogical and makes no claim of a new algorithm or convergence rate. A selective reading list covers variance reduction, compositional optimization, sign-based optimization, and minimax problems.
